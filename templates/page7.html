Page 7: Pie Chart

Showing the distribution of mental health conditions across all participants.

What the Pie Chart Represents

This pie chart shows the proportion of participants reporting each mental health condition in the dataset. The five categories are: None, Anxiety, Depression, Bipolar, and PTSD. This gives a clear picture of how common each condition is within the 2,405 participants analysed.

Code Used

Code Explanation: value_counts() counts how many participants fall into each mental health condition category. ax.pie() draws the chart where the size of each slice is proportional to its count. autopct='%1.1f%%' automatically calculates and displays the percentage for each slice to one decimal place. startangle=140 rotates the chart so the slices are arranged in a readable position.

Finding

The pie chart shows that mental health conditions are fairly evenly distributed across the dataset, with no single condition dominating. The relatively even split means that comparisons between conditions in the bar graph are fair and reliable.

Interpretation: The dataset captures a diverse range of mental health conditions with no major imbalance between groups. This makes comparisons between conditions in the bar graph more reliable, as each group has sufficient data to calculate a meaningful average happiness score.
