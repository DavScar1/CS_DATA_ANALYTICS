Page 6: Bar Graph

Comparing average happiness scores across different mental health conditions.

What the Bar Graph Represents

This bar graph shows the average happiness score for each mental health condition reported in the dataset. The conditions are: None, Anxiety, Depression, Bipolar, and PTSD. Each bar represents the mean happiness score for all participants with that condition.

Code Used

Code Explanation: groupby() groups all participants by their mental health condition, and mean() calculates the average happiness score for each group. sort_values(ascending=False) orders the conditions from highest to lowest average happiness so the bar graph is easier to read. ax.bar() draws one bar per condition, with each bar coloured using the palette dictionary defined at the top of the file.

Finding

Participants reporting no mental health condition had the highest average happiness score, while those with Depression had the lowest. This is the most meaningful pattern found across all charts in this project.

Pattern

The differences between conditions are relatively small (approximately 0.4 points), suggesting that while mental health condition is associated with happiness, the relationship is not dramatically large, possibly because happiness is influenced by many other factors simultaneously.

Interpretation: Mental health condition shows the clearest relationship with happiness score of all variables analysed. Participants with no diagnosed condition are measurably happier on average than those with depression, bipolar disorder, anxiety, or PTSD. This supports existing research linking mental health diagnoses to reduced wellbeing.
